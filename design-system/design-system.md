# KinReady Design System

## 1. Brand Identity
KinReady is a family readiness and life event guidance system. We reduce overwhelm during life’s hardest moments by providing clear, step-by-step guidance.

**Tagline**: "Know what to do next."

### Visual Metaphor
Our design is centered on **The Guided Path**. We replace intimidation and complexity with clarity and gentle direction. The core metaphor is a journey from confusion to confidence.

### Core Principles
- **Guidance Over Paperwork**: Focus on the steps and actions, not just the documents.
- **Warmth & Comfort**: Soft edges, calm colors, and inclusive imagery.
- **Clarity Through Chaos**: Use whitespace and structured layouts to reduce cognitive load.
- **Trustworthy, Not Institutional**: Avoid legal-office aesthetics (no gavels, no scales, no heavy serif fonts).

## 2. Product Ecosystem
- **KinReady Score**: A visual representation of a family's preparation status.
- **Plans**: Guided workflows for life events (e.g., "The First 72 Hours").
- **Vault**: Secure storage for essential family information.
- **Docs**: Template builders for non-legal guidance.
- **Steps**: Actionable, bite-sized tasks.
- **Learn**: Educational content and plain-language guides.
- **Scripts**: Phone scripts for navigating difficult conversations.
- **Care**: Resources for caregiving and support.

## 3. Color Palette
Designed to be soothing and professional, avoiding "emergency red" or "legal grey."

| Color | Name | Hex | Usage |
|-------|------|-----|-------|
| Primary | **KinReady Navy** | `#2C3E50` | Primary text, deep accents, navigation |
| Accent | **Sage Green** | `#82A67D` | Progress, "Success" states, growth |
| Guidance | **Calamity Blue** | `#5DADE2` | Primary buttons, action items, paths |
| Neutral | **Warm Slate** | `#7F8C8D` | Secondary text, borders, icons |
| Background | **Soft Sand** | `#F4F1EA` | Main background, page sections |
| Contrast | **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Card backgrounds, high-contrast areas |

## 4. Typography
- **Headings**: `Inter` (Extra Bold/Semi-Bold) - Modern, clear, and non-intimidating.
- **Body**: `Open Sans` or `Inter` - High legibility, especially on mobile.
- **Subtitles**: `Playfair Display` (Italic) - Used sparingly for a "human" and "thoughtful" touch.

## 5. UI Components (Mobile-First)
- **Action Cards**: Large, rounded (12px), with clear "Next Step" buttons.
- **Progress Indicators**: Soft circular or linear trackers using Sage Green.
- **The 72-Hour Guide**: A high-priority dashboard widget designed for immediate action.
- **Accessibility**: Minimum tap targets 44x44px. WCAG AA compliant contrast.
