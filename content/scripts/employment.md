# Phone Script: Employers and HR Departments

Contacting an employer is important for handling final pay, life insurance benefits provided by the company, and retirement account (401k) details.

### Information to Gather Before Calling
- The deceased person’s full legal name and Employee ID (if known).
- Their Date of Birth and Social Security Number.
- Your relationship to them.

### What to Say
"Hello, I am calling to report the passing of [Deceased Name], who was an employee at your company. Could you please connect me with the Human Resources department or the benefits coordinator?"

[Once connected]
"My name is [Your Name], and I am the [Spouse/Executor] for [Deceased Name]. I am calling to notify the company of their passing and to inquire about the process for settling their final affairs. This includes final paychecks, any accrued vacation time, and any life insurance or retirement benefits they may have had through the company."

### Common Responses
- **"We need to see the 'Letters of Testamentary'."** (This is a court document naming the Executor).
  - *Your Response:* "I am currently working with the court to obtain those. In the meantime, is there a benefits summary you can send me so I know what to expect?"
- **"They have a 401(k) through a third party."**
  - *Your Response:* "Could you provide the name of that company and a contact number so I can reach out to them directly?"

### Follow-Up Steps
1.  **Request a Benefits Package:** Ask HR to mail or email a full package of the benefits the deceased was entitled to.
2.  **Verify Life Insurance:** Many people have life insurance through work without realizing it. Always ask if there was a policy in place.
3.  **Ask about COBRA:** If you were covered under the deceased’s health insurance, ask HR about your options for continuing coverage.
