# Phone Script: Banks and Financial Institutions

When a loved one passes away, notifying their bank is a key step in securing their estate. Most banks have a dedicated "Bereavement" or "Estate" department to help you.

### Information to Gather Before Calling
- The deceased person's full legal name and Date of Birth.
- Their Social Security Number.
- Account numbers (if you have them).
- Your relationship to them (e.g., Spouse, Executor, Child).
- Have a notebook and pen ready.

### What to Say
"Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I am calling to report the passing of an account holder, [Deceased Name]. I am the [Executor / Next of Kin]. Could you please transfer me to your bereavement or estate department?"

[Once transferred]
"I need to know the next steps for [Deceased Name]’s accounts. I have their Social Security Number and Date of Birth ready. Can you tell me what documentation you require to [close the account / transition it to a survivor]? Do you need a certified death certificate or letters of testamentary?"

### Common Responses
- **"We need to freeze the account immediately."** 
  - *Your Response:* "I understand. Before you do, can you tell me if there are any automatic payments scheduled for the mortgage or utilities so I can plan for those?"
- **"We can only speak to the Executor."**
  - *Your Response:* "I am in the process of being named Executor. What documentation do I need to send you once I have it to be recognized as the point of contact?"

### Follow-Up Steps
1.  **Note the Case Number:** Ask the representative for a case or reference number for the call.
2.  **Verify the Address:** Confirm where you should mail or upload the required documents.
3.  **Check for Beneficiaries:** Ask if the account has a "Pay on Death" (POD) beneficiary listed, as this can speed up the process.
