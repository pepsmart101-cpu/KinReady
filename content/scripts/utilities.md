# Phone Script: Utilities (Power, Water, Gas, Internet)

Keeping the lights on and the home secure is a priority after a loss. Most utility companies are familiar with these calls and can help you transition the account or set a future shut-off date.

### Information to Gather Before Calling
- The service address.
- The account holder’s name and Date of Birth.
- The account number (found on a recent bill).
- The date you would like any changes to take effect.

### What to Say
"Hello, I am calling regarding the [Electric/Water/Gas] account for [Service Address]. The current account holder, [Deceased Name], has passed away. I am the [Next of Kin/Executor] and I am calling to manage the account."

**If you want to keep the service on (e.g., for a surviving spouse):**
"I would like to transfer this account into my name. Can you tell me what information or documentation you need to make that change without interrupting the service?"

**If you want to schedule a future shut-off:**
"I need to keep the utilities running until [Date], at which point we will be closing the home. Can you help me schedule a final reading and shut-off for that date?"

### Common Responses
- **"We need a copy of the death certificate."**
  - *Your Response:* "I have those ready. Is there an email address or an online portal where I can upload a digital copy?"
- **"The account has a credit balance."**
  - *Your Response:* "Thank you for letting me know. Who should I speak with about having that credit issued to the Estate of [Deceased Name]?"

### Follow-Up Steps
1.  **Stop Auto-Pay:** If the account was on auto-pay from a bank account that is now frozen, make sure to provide a new payment method so the service isn't cut off unexpectedly.
2.  **Confirm the Final Bill:** Ask where the final bill should be mailed.
