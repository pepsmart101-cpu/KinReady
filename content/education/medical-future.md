# Deciding Your Medical Future: Who Speaks for You?

If you were in an accident and couldn't speak, who would doctors look to for answers? Choosing your "Medical Voice" is one of the most important parts of family readiness.

### The Two Pieces of Your Plan

#### 1. Your Medical Voice (Healthcare Proxy)
This is a person you choose to make medical decisions if you can't.
*   **Next Step:** Pick someone who knows your values and can stay calm when things are stressful. Have a 5-minute conversation with them today to ask if they’re willing to take on this role.

#### 2. Your Care Wishes (Living Will)
While your Medical Voice makes the choices, your Care Wishes document tells them what you’d want. It covers things like ventilators or other life-support measures.
*   **Why it Matters:** It takes the "guilt" out of the decision for your loved ones. They aren't "choosing" for you; they are simply following your instructions.

### Making it Official
You don't need a complicated legal process to start. Most states have simple, free forms you can fill out. Once you've chosen your Medical Voice, the next step is to let your doctor know.
