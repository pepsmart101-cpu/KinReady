# Family Readiness: Building Your Roadmap

Family readiness is about making sure your loved ones aren't left in the dark during an emergency or a sudden life change. It’s not about paperwork—it’s about peace of mind.

### What is a "Ready" Family?
A ready family has a clear understanding of "what to do next" if something unexpected happens. This includes:

#### 1. The Emergency Plan
If you were suddenly hospitalized, would your family know which bills are due tomorrow? A ready family has a list of essential accounts and a plan for how to keep the household running.

#### 2. The Decision Maker
Who speaks for you if you can't? Family readiness means naming a trusted person to handle your finances and make medical choices on your behalf. We call this choosing your "Next-of-Kin Advocate."

#### 3. The Digital Handover
From family photos to utility logins, so much of our life is online. A readiness plan includes a secure way for your family to access your digital world so nothing is lost.

#### 4. The Guidance Document
While a "Will" is a part of the puzzle, a true readiness plan includes a "Letter of Instruction"—a simple note telling your family where the keys are, how to care for the dog, and what your final wishes look like.

### Your Next Step
Don't try to build the whole roadmap today. Start with one thing: **Who is your emergency contact?** Make sure they know they are the ones you trust to take the lead.
