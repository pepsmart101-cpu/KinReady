# Getting Your Basics in Order: The Readiness Toolkit

You don't need a law degree to protect your family. Being "ready" just means having a few key tools in place so your loved ones know exactly what to do if you’re ever unable to tell them.

### Your Essential Toolkit

#### 1. The Decision Maker (Power of Attorney)
This is a simple step where you choose a person you trust to handle things like paying your bills or managing your home if you’re sick or in the hospital. Without this, your family might have to ask a court for permission to help you.

#### 2. The Medical Voice (Healthcare Proxy)
If you can't speak for yourself in a medical emergency, who should? Naming a "Medical Voice" ensures your healthcare wishes are followed and takes the stress off your family's shoulders.

#### 3. The Family Guardian (Guardianship)
If you have children, the most important part of your plan is naming who would care for them. This is usually documented in a "Will," but it's really about your children's future and security.

#### 4. The Handover Plan (Beneficiaries)
Many accounts—like life insurance or retirement—allow you to name a person to receive the funds directly. This is the fastest way to get support to your family. Check these accounts today to make sure the names are correct.

### Why This Matters Right Now
Life is unpredictable. Having these basics in order isn't about the end of life—it's about making sure the "next step" is clear for the people you love most.
