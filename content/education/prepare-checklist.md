# Your "First 5" Readiness Checklist

Welcome to KinReady. You’re giving your family an incredible gift by planning ahead. Here are your first five readiness tasks to move from "uncertain" to "prepared."

### 1. Choose Your Emergency Contact (Advocate)
This is the person you trust most to take the lead in a crisis. They don't need to be a lawyer—they just need to know they are the ones you trust.
- **Action:** Decide who this person is and let them know. Ask: *"If anything happens, can I count on you to be my primary contact?"*

### 2. Identify Your "Medical Voice"
If you can't speak for yourself in a medical emergency, who should? Naming a Healthcare Proxy ensures your values are respected.
- **Action:** Identify your proxy and have a 5-minute conversation about your general health wishes.

### 3. Secure Your Digital Access
Your photos, memories, and even your bills are online. Don't let your family get locked out of your digital life.
- **Action:** Add a "Legacy Contact" to your primary device (Apple or Google) or share your password manager's emergency kit with your advocate.

### 4. Start Your Asset Inventory
If you weren't here tomorrow, would your family know which bank accounts to check or where your life insurance policy is kept?
- **Action:** Make a simple list of your primary financial accounts and physical assets (house, car, etc.) and where the related paperwork is located.

### 5. Review Your Beneficiaries
Some accounts (like life insurance or 401ks) go directly to the person you named on the account, regardless of what your Will says.
- **Action:** Check your primary accounts today to ensure your beneficiary designations are up to date.

---
**Your Goal:** Complete just ONE of these today. Family readiness isn't built in an afternoon; it’s built one small step at a time.
