# Creating a Digital Legacy Plan for Your Family

We spend hours every day online, yet most of us haven't thought about what happens to our "digital life" when we’re gone. Without a plan, your family could lose access to precious photos, important financial records, and even your social media memories.

### What is a Digital Legacy?
Your digital legacy is everything you own or manage online. It includes:
- **Financial:** Online banking, PayPal, utility accounts, and investment apps.
- **Memories:** Photos in the cloud (Google Photos, iCloud) and social media profiles.
- **Communication:** Email accounts and messaging apps.
- **Assets:** Domain names, websites, and even "hidden" assets like cryptocurrency.
- **Subscriptions:** Streaming services, software, and digital publications.

### 3 Steps to Secure Your Digital Legacy

#### 1. Use a Password Manager
The easiest gift you can give your family is a way to log in. Use a password manager (like 1Password or Bitwarden) and ensure your "Next-of-Kin Advocate" has the master key or access to your emergency kit.

#### 2. Name Your "Legacy Contacts"
Many major platforms now have built-in tools for your digital legacy:
- **Apple:** Set up a "Legacy Contact" in your iPhone settings. They’ll get access to your photos and data after you pass.
- **Google:** Use the "Inactive Account Manager" to decide who should get access to your Gmail or Photos if you haven't logged in for a while.
- **Facebook:** Choose a "Legacy Contact" to manage your profile or turn it into a memorial page.

#### 3. List Your "Invisible" Bills and Assets
Some bills and assets only exist in your inbox or on your phone. 
- **Subscriptions:** Make a list of your monthly automatic payments so your family can cancel them if needed.
- **Crypto & Wallets:** If you have cryptocurrency, your family needs to know where the keys (or seed phrases) are kept. Without these, the assets are lost forever.

### Your Next Step
Open your phone settings today and add one "Legacy Contact." It takes less than two minutes but can save your family months of frustration.
