# The First 72 Hours: A Guide After Loss

The first few days after losing a loved one are incredibly difficult. KinReady is here to help you focus on what’s most important. Here is your step-by-step guidance for the first 72 hours.

### Step 1: Immediate Care (Hours 1–6)
*   **The Pronouncement:** If the death happened at home, call 911 or their doctor so a legal pronouncement can be made. If in a hospital or hospice, the staff will handle this.
*   **The Notification:** Contact two or three close family members or friends. Ask one of them to be your "Communication Lead" to help notify others.

### Step 2: The Next 24 Hours
*   **Dependents & Pets:** Ensure any children, elderly family members, or pets are cared for immediately.
*   **Secure the Home:** If your loved one lived alone, ensure the home is locked and any vehicles are parked in a safe place. 
*   **Locate Instructions:** Check if they left a "Letter of Instruction" or had pre-arranged funeral or burial plans.

### Step 3: Days 2 and 3
*   **Choose a Funeral Home:** They will help you with the transportation of the body and start the process for the **Death Certificate**.
*   **Order Death Certificates:** Ask for at least 10–15 copies. You will need these for almost every "next step" (banks, insurance, etc.).
*   **Contact the Employer:** If they were working, let their HR department know. They can often help with final pay and life insurance info.

### Your "Right Now" Task
Go to the kitchen, drink a glass of water, and find one person who can sit with you while you make the first few phone calls. You don't have to do this alone.
