# KinReady Onboarding: Guided Support from Day One

**Headline:** Facing a loss? We’re here to help you through the next 72 hours.
**Body:** If you’re navigating the immediate aftermath of a loved one's passing, you don't have to do it alone. We’ll provide a step-by-step checklist for the first few days so you can focus on your family, not the paperwork.
**Button:** Help me through a loss
**Secondary Link:** I’m here to prepare for the future (Family Readiness)

## Screen 2: The Proactive Path (Family Readiness)
**Headline:** Give your family the gift of a clear plan.
**Body:** KinReady is your family readiness system. We help you organize your life event guidance—from medical wishes to financial access—so your loved ones never have to guess what to do next in an emergency.
**Button:** Build my readiness plan

## Screen 3: Finding Your Starting Point
**Headline:** Let’s find your next step.
**Body:** Whether you’re in a crisis or planning ahead, we’ll ask a few questions to identify your most urgent needs. This helps us build your custom family readiness checklist.
**Button:** Start my guidance

## Assessment Questions (Crisis Mode)
1. **Immediate Tasks:** Has a legal pronouncement of death been made by a professional? (Yes / No / I don’t know how)
2. **Support System:** Do you have a list of close family and friends who need to be notified today? (Yes / No / Working on it)
3. **Wishes:** Are you aware of any pre-arranged funeral or burial wishes? (Yes / No / I need help finding them)

## Assessment Questions (Prep Mode)
1. **Emergency Access:** If you were in the hospital today, does someone you trust know where to find your vital info? (Yes / No / Partially)
2. **Health Voice:** Have you named someone to speak for you if you can't make medical decisions? (Yes / No / What is this?)
3. **Digital Access:** Does your family have a way to access your digital accounts or pay bills in an emergency? (Yes / No / Only some)
